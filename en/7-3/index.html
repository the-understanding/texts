      • Correlation
        • Correlation coefficient

        • Prediction interval, Tolerance interval
        • Multicollinearity

        • Dimensionality reduction

      • Generalized linear mixed model

    • Bayesian statistics

        • Prior probability (Prior distribution), Posterior probability, Bayesian updating

    7.3.2 Causal Inference

    • Causal inference
    • Causality (→ 4.2.2 Causality)
    • Potential outcomes model (Rubin causal model)
      • Average treatment effect
      • Potential effect model, Counterfactual model
        • Propensity score matching
      • Counterfactual machine learning
    • Structural causal model (Pearl causal model)
      • Directed acyclic graph, do-calculus, backdoor criterion, frontdoor criterion, adjustment formula
    • Instrumental variables estimation
    • Inverse probability weighting, Causal forest, Difference in differences
    • Bayesian network
    • Strength of evidence in Epidemiology research (Evidence-based medicine), Randomized controlled trial, Double-blind study
    • Bradford Hill criteria
    • Granger causality
    • Causal mediation analysis
