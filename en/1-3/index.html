    1.3 Perception and Recognition

    1.3.1 Perception

    • Sense and Reaction
      • Special senses
        • Visual perception, Hearing, Taste, Sense of smell, Sense of balance, Sense organ, Five senses
      • Somatosensory system
        • Haptic perception (Tactile sense), Thermoception (heat and cold), Pain, Mouthfeel, Tickling, Itch, etc.
        • Deep sensation
          • Kinesthesia
          • Sense of balance, Pallesthesia (vibration sense)
          • Proprioception
      • Illusion
      • Physiological reaction
        • Reflex

    1.3.2 Recognition

    • Recognition
      • Cognition, Epistemology, Consciousness, Experience, Pure experience, Attention / Attention (machine learning), Salience (neuroscience)
    • Epistemology
      • Recognition process: Information generation from object, sensory input, information processing in the brain, comparison with memory, abstraction, conceptualization, verbalization/expression
      • Pattern recognition
        • Graphic recognition
        • Recognition of similarity, Analogy
      • Spatial ability
      • Embodied cognition
      • Highly accurate recognition through experience
      • Addition of meaning by background/experiential knowledge
      • Theory-ladenness (→ 6.2.1 Scientific method)

    • A priori and a posteriori
    • Qualia
    • Influence of Emotion
