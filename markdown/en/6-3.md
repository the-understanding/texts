## **6.3 Design of [Experimental](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Experiment) Systems**
### **6.3.1 [System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/System), [Factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Factor_(experimental_design)), [Result](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Result)**
- Factors (Groups) / Results (Groups)
    - Substance: Components, cofactors, amount, etc.
    - Conditions: Location, time/timing, temperature/humidity/field, etc.
    - [Event](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Event_(philosophy)) / Relationship: Change in motion, change in form, change in quantity, reaction, combination, decomposition, change in implication, etc.
    - Level, quantified observed variables
    - [Latent variable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latent_variable)
- Characteristics of Factors
    - Function: Effect on other factors, environment or timing in which the function appears
    - Property: Shape, amount, etc., changes/influences from other factors
    - Origin/Future: Source, evolution, differentiation, integration, etc.
- System Patterns and Rules
    - Relationships between factors
    - System rules, patterns, purpose
    - Response when rules are broken
- Applicability
    - Modification of factors, changing conditions, new combinations
    - Application of rules, new rules
    - Scale up/down, etc.

### **6.3.6 Confirming the Relationship between Factor (Group) A and Result (Group) B (Reference)**
- Is A really a factor?
    - Is it a [Coincidence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coincidence)? (Check [Reproducibility](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reproducibility), Uniformity of Nature, Universal Validity)
        - Can it be repeatedly reproduced? Can it be reproduced by other researchers/equipment/locations?
        - Can it be confirmed by another measurement method?
    - Is A always present whenever B occurs (exists)?
        - Does A always precede on the time axis? (Confirmation of time axis)
        - Does eliminating A consistently prevent B from occurring? (Control group, counterfactual check)
        - Does introducing A into a state without A cause B to occur? (Confirmation of reconstitution 1)
    - Is there a [Correlation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Correlation) in quantity between B and A? (Confirmation of correlation)
    	- If there's an inverse correlation, does A act to suppress B?
    - Is there a factor (group) C that is a common cause for both A and B? (Latent factor, check for [Confounding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confounding))
    - Is there a result (group) D that is a common result of both A and B? (Check for collider bias)
    - Is there a possibility that B is a factor of A? (Denial of reverse causality)
        - [Interaction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interaction), Synergy ([Synergy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Synergy)), [Feedback](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feedback)
    - Is there a possibility that A and B coincide (are different perspectives on the same phenomenon)? (Denial of equivalence)
- Can factor A be decomposed? (Main cause, feature extraction)
    - Are there cases where A exists but B does not? In what situations?
    - Are there ranges/conditions of A that cause B when the factor level is changed?
    - Can A be replaced with something similar, partially modified, etc.?
    - Can the relationships between factor groups be grasped?
        - Cross-loading
- Is A a direct factor? (Confirmation of indirect factors)
    - Is it possible that A acts on factor (group) C, and C is the direct factor for B?
        - Is there a direct relationship between A and B? (Confirmation of binding/signal transduction)
    - Are other factors like cofactors, promoters, or inhibitors involved? Can the system be scaled up/down?
    - Is the model overfitted due to data selection bias? Is cross-validation possible?
    - Does A affect things other than B? (Presence of observed variables other than B, is it breaking the system itself?) (Confirmation of system suitability)
    - Even if A → C and C → B can be shown, when A, B, and C exist simultaneously, could it be that A interferes with C → B, or B interferes with A → C, preventing the A → B → C sequence?
- Is A the only factor for B? (Confirmation of specificity)
    - Can it be confirmed in a different system (different place, time, etc.) that A alone causes B?
        - Reconstitution
    - Are there other potential factors besides A? Can B be reproduced with something other than A?
        - [Reverse engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reverse_engineering)
- Does it contradict existing knowledge? Could it be a new discovery? (Confirmation of theoretical validity)
