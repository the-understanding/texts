- [Complexity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complexity)
- Difficulty in integrated understanding and control of micro and macro phenomena
    - [Microeconomics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microeconomics), [Macroeconomics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macroeconomics)
    - [Mesoscopic physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mesoscopic_physics)
- Difficulty in unifying rules
    - [Diversity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diversity_(sociology)), Consideration for [Minority group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minority_group)
    - [House rule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_rule) (Local rule)
- Conflict between maintaining overall order and maximizing individual freedom
    - [Totalitarianism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Totalitarianism)
    - [Individualism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Individualism)
    - [Liberalism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberalism)
        - [Rational choice theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rational_choice_theory)
        - [Tragedy of the commons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tragedy_of_the_commons)

    - Trade-off between efficiency and flexibility
- [Second law of thermodynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_law_of_thermodynamics) and control/maintenance
    - [Degrees of freedom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Degrees_of_freedom)
- Unpredictability
    - [Many-body problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Many-body_problem), Many-body system
    - [Chaos theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaos_theory)
    - [Butterfly effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Butterfly_effect)

- Complexity and maintenance
    - Possibility of a partial modification affecting other unintended parts
        - [Spaghetti code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spaghetti_code)
    - Cost and risk of changes and modifications
    - Untestability
