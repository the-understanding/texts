## **5.2 Structure of a System**
- Purpose and role achieved as an [Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organization) or [System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/System)
- System framework
    - [Software framework](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_framework), [Template](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Template), [Library (computing)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Library_(computing))
    - [Affordance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Affordance)
- Inflow, outflow, and accumulation to/from the system
- System [Boundary (topology)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boundary_(topology)), [Field (physics)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Field_(physics)), [Environment (systems)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environment_(systems))
- [Element (category theory)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Element_(category_theory)), [Modular design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modular_design)
    - Major components, triggers for change or movement, core
    - Near-decomposability
- [Interaction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interaction) between elements
- Mechanical interaction, [Phase space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phase_space)
- [Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organization)
    - [Category:Organizational theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Category:Organizational_theory)
    - Vertical organization, horizontal organization, [Hierarchical organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hierarchical_organization), [Informal organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Informal_organization)
    - [Matrix management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matrix_management)
- Collection of events and things
    - [Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society), [Economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economy), Ecosystem
        - [Category:Systems ecology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Category:Systems_ecology)
- Information system / [Category:Conceptual systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Category:Conceptual_systems)
    - [Language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Language), [Law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law), [Rule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rule), [Formal system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formal_system), [Ideology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ideology)
- Perfect information system, imperfect information system, [Information asymmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_asymmetry)
