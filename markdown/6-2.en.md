### **6.2.4 Steps for [Hypothesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypothesis) Verification**
  - Setting research themes/issues
  - Hypothesis building
  - Determining methods for collecting data for verification
  - [Experiment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Experiment), data collection, selection, preprocessing
  - Building a [Scientific model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_modelling), estimating parameters
  - Evaluation, verification, and modification of the obtained model
  - Utilization of the model / Generation of new models
