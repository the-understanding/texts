## Afterword

&emsp;Understanding is much like driving stakes into the sea. Humans desperately attempt to expand their tiny territory of understanding floating in the vast ocean by driving stakes into key shallow spots. After careful consideration, a location is decided and a stake is driven; just when one thinks it is safe to expand the land, an unexpected massive wave sometimes arrives and sweeps the stake away from its roots, forcing one to reconsider and drive the stake again. I feel that humans are compelled to repeat this process in this world filled with information.

&emsp;The background behind creating this summary stems from recalling that what is called rational information processing undergoes the process outlined in Chapters 1 through 9, and feeling the need for a checklist to verify whether oversights, biases, or the like have not occurred during that process.

&emsp;In this summary, human thought is simplified and condensed into two major information processing mechanisms: grouping (including deduction, induction, and causal relationships) and counting (including abduction, simulation, and computation). Grouping is the organization of information, but organizing information is the structuring of information, structuring information is the compression of information, and compressing information is the improvement of information usability. Counting is prediction, serving as material to judge whether a result is desirable or not, and providing the rationale that determines the next action. The influence of embodiment, experience, and values on thought is not described in detail here, as it belongs to the domain of human nature.

&emsp;In an era where many tasks leave human hands to rely on AI and devices, this summary was compiled in this format, focusing on basic content, in order to grasp an overall view of handling information (organizing, generating) as a common concept. Details of individual parts are not included here because numerous excellent materials exist elsewhere, but it is hoped that looking over the whole will yield new insights. Although this summary itself is very unrefined, I hope that better works will follow, enabling human thought to expand even further.
