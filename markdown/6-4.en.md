### **6.4.1 Management of Analytical Methods**
- Equipment/Reagent management: Qualification

### **6.4.2 Measurement Controls**
- [Type I and type II errors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_I_and_type_II_errors), [Sensitivity and specificity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sensitivity_and_specificity)
- Positive control: Confirmation of false negatives and non-sample factors
	- System suitability
	- Sample suitability
- Negative control: Confirmation of false positives and non-sample factors
	- Blank control: Confirmation of background noise
	- Negative control excluding only factor (group) A
	- Confirmation of effect on system using dominant-negative control
- Standard products, [Certified reference materials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Certified_reference_materials), Working standards
- Timing of control sample measurements
	- Interpolation: Assuming measurements of samples placed between control samples are fine

### **6.4.3 [Calibration curve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calibration_curve)**
- Modeling and fitting
	- Mathematical models derived from principles
		- Probabilistic models
		- Continuous change at a constant rate
		- Continuous change at a constant proportion
		- Changes in equilibrium reactions, etc.
	- Data-driven calibration curve models: Described in Chapter 7
- Interpolation between data points
- Limit of Quantitation (LOQ): Relationship between level and measurement precision
- Limit of Detection (LOD): [Signal-to-noise ratio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Signal-to-noise_ratio)
- Evaluation of calibration curve: [Coefficient of determination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coefficient_of_determination)

### **6.4.4 [Measurement uncertainty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measurement_uncertainty), [Observational error](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Observational_error), and [Error](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Error)**
#### **6.4.4.1 [Observational error](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Observational_error)**
- Systematic error, [Bias](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bias), Selection [Bias](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bias)
	- Trueness and Accuracy
		- A value considered to have one true value
		- A value considered to inherently have a distribution: Population measurement
	- Management and [Calibration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calibration) of measurement equipment and materials
		- [Sensitivity (electronics)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sensitivity_(electronics)), Sensitivity coefficient
		- Lot-to-lot variation
	- Interference: Presence of factors affecting measurement results
	- Theoretical precision degradation rate, precision limits
- Random error, [Statistical dispersion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_dispersion)
	- [Normal distribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normal_distribution), Normality, [Homoscedasticity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homoscedasticity_and_heteroscedasticity), [Standard deviation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_deviation)
- Estimation of repeatability
	- Standard deviation
	- (Least) squared error for regression models
- [Noise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noise)
- [Outlier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outlier)
- Tolerance
	- [Significant figures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Significant_figures)
	- [Approximation error](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Approximation_error)

#### **6.4.4.3 Grasping and Reducing Errors**
- Identifying and improving error sources
- Grasping error distribution through repeated data measurement, etc., and using statistics
- Regression models using the least squares method and error reduction
- Evaluation of reproducibility: Changing factors such as equipment, location, time, sample, person, or another researcher
- Simultaneous measurement of absolute and relative reference samples

- [Data analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_analysis), model building, parameter estimation, learning from data (parameter tuning)

- Model utilization, generation
    - Inputting new data and evaluating/using the output
- [Data preprocessing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_preprocessing): [Data wrangling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_wrangling), [Data cleansing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_cleansing), [Exception handling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exception_handling), [Outlier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outlier), [Singularity (mathematics)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singularity_(mathematics)), [Missing data](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missing_data), [Feature extraction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feature_extraction), [Normalization (statistics)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normalization_(statistics)), Parameter weighting

    - [Garbage in, garbage out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garbage_in,_garbage_out)
    - Occurrence of data bias, failure in anomaly (opportunity) detection
    - Loss of reproducibility
