## **7.2 Mathematical [Modeling (Scientific)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_modelling)**

### **7.2.1 Classification Axes of Mathematical Models**
- Deterministic models, Stochastic models (Probabilistic models)
- Static models / Dynamic models
- [Discriminative model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discriminative_model), [Generative model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generative_model)
- Mechanistic models / Data-driven models (→ 6.4.3 Calibration curve)
- Interpretability, [Black box](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_box) / White box (→ 7.4.1.6 Interpretability and Uncertainty)
- [Demand-driven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demand-driven), [Data-driven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data-driven)
- Qualitative models ([Qualitative research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qualitative_research)), Quantitative models ([Quantitative research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantitative_research)) (→ 6.2.2 Empirical research)

### **7.2.2 Typology by Model Construction Procedure**
- [Mathematical model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_model) (→ 6.2.1 Scientific method), [Governing equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governing_equation) (Fundamental equation)
- Models that regress (learn) data and arrive at linear equations
    - [Statistical model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_model) (→ 7.3.1 Statistical models)
    - [Statistical inference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_inference)
    - Rule-based, [Expert system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expert_system)
    - [Machine learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Machine_learning)
        - [Supervised learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supervised_learning), [Self-supervised learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-supervised_learning), [Unsupervised learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unsupervised_learning)
    - [Deep learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deep_learning), [Neural network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neural_network)
    - [Artificial general intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_general_intelligence) (→ 10.4.3 AI safety and social impact)
- Models described by differential equations, where each step is simulated by numerical calculation
    - [Nonlinear systems theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonlinear_system), [Nonlinear physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonlinear_dynamics)
    - [Numerical analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numerical_analysis), [Simulation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simulation) (→ 10.2.2 Algorithms)
        - [State space (control theory)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State-space_representation)
        - [Initial value problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Initial_value_problem), [Boundary value problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boundary_value_problem), [Butterfly effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Butterfly_effect)
    - [Agent-based model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agent-based_model) (→ 7.3.4 Game theory)
        - [Evolutionary programming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evolutionary_programming)
    - [Pseudorandom number generator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudorandom_number_generator) (→ 6.3.4 Search space and data sampling design)
