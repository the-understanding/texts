## **9.4 Failure of Discussion and Consensus Building**
### **9.4.1 Differences in Opinion**
- Lack of information, prediction gaps
    - Insufficient analysis time, lack of discussion for out-of-scenario situations, indecisiveness
    - Scoring extremely high points for risks/benefits that have a very small probability of occurring
- Misalignment of purposes
    - The group has not unified its intentions
    - Unclear goals
- Misalignment of values
    - Differences in how weight is placed on meaning
    - Different priorities regarding for whom and for what
    - Differences in standards of [Equality (mathematics)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equality_(mathematics)) (Note: Linked closest concept for Equality), [Fairness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justice_(economics)), and [Justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justice)
- Argumentum ad populum (Violence of numbers)
    - [Deliberative democracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deliberative_democracy)
